About Us

Founded by Aditya Ahuja and John Xie, Neighborhood Nexus Technology & Community Cooperative is a worker-owned organization dedicated to creating local jobs, supporting education, and increasing technology access. Every employee has a voice in decisions, and all profits are reinvested into the neighborhood to benefit our community.
